System Architecture & Features

Engineered for real-world automotive telemetry extraction, automated diagnostic reasoning, and predictive maintenance.

Real-Time OBD-II Telemetry

Continuous monitoring of critical vehicle PIDs: vehicle speed, engine RPM, coolant temperature, alternator battery voltage, fuel tank level, engine load, and intake air temperature.

Isolation Forest ML Anomaly Engine

Trained on healthy driving telemetry distributions. Detects subtle multidimensional outliers (e.g. RPM jitter vs throttle mismatch, voltage drops, heat spikes) running in Python backend.

Predictive Maintenance Alerts

Translates detected sensor deviations into actionable automotive repair insights with severity levels (Healthy, Warning, Critical) and official Diagnostic Trouble Codes (e.g. P0217, P0562, P0300).

Historical Reports & CSV Export

All telemetry snapshots are indexed into an embedded SQLite database. Search, paginate by status, and export complete drive cycle logs in standard CSV format for fleet analytics.

OBD-II Hardware Integration & Architecture

Plug & Play Ready

The system implements a clean Hardware Abstraction Layer (obd_hardware.py). When physical OBD-II hardware (such as an ELM327 Bluetooth/USB dongle or STN1110 scanner) is connected to the car's 16-pin DLC port, it queries live standard SAE J1979 PIDs.

In development, classroom, or demo environments without vehicle access, the built-in Virtual CAN Bus Simulator generates synchronized multi-sensor drive cycles with realistic transmission gear ratios, thermal warm-up curves, and fault injection capabilities.

Standard Monitored OBD-II PIDs:

  • PID 0x0D: Vehicle Speed km/h (0 - 255)
  • PID 0x0C: Engine RPM RPM (0 - 16,383)
  • PID 0x05: Engine Coolant Temperature °C (-40 to 215)
  • PID 0x42: Control Module Voltage Volts (0 - 65.5V)
  • PID 0x2F: Fuel Tank Level Input % (0 - 100%)
